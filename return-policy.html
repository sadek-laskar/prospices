Return Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Spices are food products, so for hygiene and safety we cannot accept the return of opened or used items. We will always put things right if we made a mistake or your order arrived damaged.

When You Can Return or Replace

You may ask for a replacement or refund if:

  • your parcel arrived damaged, leaking or tampered with;
  • you received the wrong product, weight or quantity; or
  • the product is past its stated expiry date or is spoiled.

What We Cannot Accept

  • Packs that have been opened, used or partly used, unless the product is faulty.
  • Returns because of a change of mind or a difference in taste preference.
  • Products damaged after delivery through storage or handling.
  • Custom, private-label or bulk orders made to your specification, unless they do not match the agreed specification.

How to Raise a Claim

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Share your name, order details and clear photos or a short video of the product, the pack and the outer packaging.
  3. We will review it and reply, usually within 2 working days.

Replacements and Refunds

If your claim is approved, we will send a replacement or refund the amount, whichever you prefer where stock allows. Refunds are made to your original payment method and usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days after approval. We may ask you to return the item, in which case we will arrange pick-up or tell you how.

Cancelling an Order

You can cancel before your order is dispatched by contacting us, and we will refund any payment made. Once an order has been shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

Your Consumer Rights

This policy does not affect your rights under Indian consumer protection law.
